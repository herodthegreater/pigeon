## Final review

Round 3 confirmed five defects, each fixed and then re-checked by two fresh skeptics trying to show it was still broken:

1. **Pigeon recipes known by their label** (HIGH): a plain cast and a per-tensor-scaled float step shared a label, and the label was the recipe's identity, so links, run history and sightings could confuse them. Recipes are now known by every setting (`recipeKey`, the link's own step text); labels name per-tensor scaling ("FP16 one scale").
2. **"Compresses" on same-size methods** (HIGH): born-again, over-parameterization and small-tricks runs now say they change how a model is trained, not its size, and hatch no bird.
3. **"1.00× smaller" for unchanged models** (LOW): the Coop boards say "same size", and shrink ratios are cut, never rounded up, so a model 1.77 times smaller reads "1.7×" beside its rock pigeon instead of the dove's 1.8.
4. **Theme after Back** (LOW): the theme and its control stay in step after a back/forward-cache restore, a reload-style Back, a change in another tab, and with site storage blocked.
5. **Status line weight** (LOW): the Coop's engine status is regular-weight mono like every other status line.

The theme fix took three passes: the control's listener now lives in the page head, so a pick works the moment the control exists, and the control and page stay in step on load, on both kinds of Back, across tabs, and with site storage blocked or full. The last confirmation pass found nothing a visitor would plausibly hit; its two residual edges (a cosmetic mismatch lasting one network round-trip, and a pick lost when storage is full) were closed as well.

The re-checks also closed two older edges: runs saved by the live Coop before this release whose labels can't say what ran (float scalings, MX/NV sizes, a 2:4 step left at 0%) are dropped when they migrate, rather than shown under a label that may be wrong.
